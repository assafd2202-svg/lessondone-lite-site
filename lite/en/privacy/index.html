Privacy Policy

LessonDone Lite · Effective October 18, 2026 · Version 1.0

LessonDone Lite is a Google Sheets add-on that helps private tutors in Israel track lesson payments and send payment reminders. Payments are detected from notifications of Bit and PayBox, Israel's leading person-to-person mobile payment apps (comparable to Venmo or Zelle in the United States). Our principle is simple: your data stays in your spreadsheet, in your Google account. We have no access to your spreadsheet and keep no copy of it. This policy lists exactly what does pass through us, why, and for how long.

1. Who we are

The service is operated by Assaf Dagan ("the Operator", "we"). Privacy contact: assafd2202@gmail.com, or WhatsApp +972-52-4470034.

2. Roles

  1. The tutor (user) installs the add-on on their own spreadsheet. For student and parent information in that spreadsheet, the tutor is the data controller: they enter it, decide what to keep and why, and are responsible towards those people.
  2. We process the minimum needed on the tutor's behalf, only to operate the service. For the tutor's own registration details (section 4.1) we are the controller.

3. What stays only with you

  • The spreadsheet itself: lessons, students, parents, phone numbers, amounts and payment history.
  • The tabs and columns the add-on creates in it.
  • The add-on's settings, stored in Google's per-user settings in your account.

The add-on runs inside your Google account with the permissions you granted. The Operator has no access to your spreadsheet and keeps no copy of its content.

5. What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent or transfer data to third parties, except the providers in section 8 as needed to run the service.
  • We don't use data for advertising, marketing, profiling or credit decisions.
  • We don't use data to train artificial-intelligence models.
  • No human reads your spreadsheet content, except when you explicitly ask for help that requires viewing specific data (then only that data, with your consent), for security purposes, or where required by law.

6. Google user data

This section describes specifically how LessonDone Lite accesses, uses, stores, shares, protects, retains and deletes Google user data.

6.1 What Google user data is accessed

  • The content of the one spreadsheet you select at installation (Google Sheets): lesson rows, student names, amounts, payer details and phone numbers that you entered, read and written inside your own Google account.
  • Your Google account email address.
  • The spreadsheet's ID, name, time zone and tab names.

6.2 How it is used

Only to provide the add-on's user-facing features: matching payments to lessons, writing paid amounts, balances and statuses, building the summary tab, and sending the payment reminders you approved. Your email address identifies your subscription.

6.3 Where it is stored

Spreadsheet content is processed inside Google Apps Script in your account and stays in your spreadsheet. It is never copied to or stored on our servers. The only Google user data we store is your email address and the spreadsheet ID and name (section 4.1), and — once, only if the layout is not recognised at installation — the spreadsheet's column headers (section 4.3).

6.4 How it is shared

Google user data is not sold, rented or shared, except: your email address and spreadsheet ID are stored with our hosting providers (Google, Cloudflare) to run the service; and, if you enable WhatsApp reminders, the parent phone number and reminder text are sent through your own GREEN API account to deliver the message you approved.

6.5 How it is protected

Encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by our providers; access limited to the Operator; per-tutor secret IDs; minimal storage (section 10).

6.6 Retention and deletion

Email address and spreadsheet ID: while the subscription is active and up to 24 months after use ends. Column headers in error reports: up to 24 months. You can request deletion at any time (section 11); we respond within 30 days. Removing the add-on and revoking access at myaccount.google.com/permissions stops all access to your spreadsheet immediately.

6.7 Limited Use and AI

LessonDone Lite's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google Workspace APIs are not used to develop, improve, or train generalized or non-personalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models. No human reads Google user data except with your explicit consent for specific data, for security purposes, or where required by law.

8. Third-party providers

The service relies on the following, each operating under its own terms and privacy policy and outside our control:

  • Google (USA and other countries) — Sheets, Apps Script, and the accounts holding your spreadsheet and our internal admin sheet (the registration data in 4.1).
  • Cloudflare (global network; database in Western Europe) — the relay server that receives payment notifications until collected, and the registration data in 4.1.
  • GREEN API — only if you connect WhatsApp (see also section 9 of the Terms — risk of a number ban). The GREEN API account is yours, under your own agreement with them. Parent phone numbers and reminder text pass through them to be sent from your number.
  • WhatsApp / Meta — the platform delivering messages to parents.
  • Bit, PayBox (Israeli person-to-person payment apps, operated by Bank Hapoalim and Discount Bank) — send the payment notification to your phone. We do not connect to them, use their APIs, or receive data from them directly.
  • MacroDroid (Android) / Apple Shortcuts (iPhone) — apps on your phone that forward the notification to the relay server.

LessonDone Lite is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Google, WhatsApp/Meta, Bit (Bank Hapoalim), PayBox (Discount Bank), GREEN API, Cloudflare, MacroDroid or Apple. All trademarks belong to their owners.

9. International transfers

The providers in section 8 operate servers outside Israel. By accepting this policy you agree to the transfer of the data described here to them, solely to operate the service.

10. Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Each tutor is identified by a unique secret ID, and admin interfaces are protected by a separate secret. We keep minimal data for minimal time. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee that data will never be exposed. Keep your personal ID private.

11. Deletion

  • Remove the add-on: Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons. Revoke access: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To delete your registration data, contact us (section 1). We respond within 30 days, except data we must keep by law.
  • Payment notifications are deleted automatically within 7 days at most.
  • The add-on writes to your spreadsheet but never deletes it. Removing the add-on does not delete the spreadsheet or what was written in it.

12. Your rights

Under the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981, you may access the data we hold about you and ask to correct or delete it. You may also contact the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority.

13. Your responsibility towards students and parents

Student, parent and payer details in your spreadsheet are your responsibility. You must have a lawful basis to hold and use them, inform parents that they may receive payment reminders from you on WhatsApp, stop messaging a parent who asks, and not send marketing content.

14. Minors

The service is intended for adult tutors. We do not collect data directly from minors.

15. Security incidents

If data under our responsibility is exposed unlawfully, we will notify affected tutors promptly, and authorities where required by law.

16. Changes

We may update this policy and will show the update date. Material changes will be announced in the add-on or by WhatsApp/email at least 14 days in advance. Changes to how data received from Google is used take effect only after renewed consent.
