How it works

Three steps, keeping the spreadsheet you already use.

1

A parent pays

In Bit or PayBox, as usual. The notification arrives on your phone.

2

Your phone forwards it

An automation app on your phone (MacroDroid on Android, Shortcuts on iPhone) sends only the notification text.

3

Recorded in your sheet

Next to the student's lesson, with the updated balance. Anything unmatched is assigned with one click.

What you get

Everything you need to know who paid, without another system to manage.

Questions

Do I need to change my spreadsheet?

No. The add-on recognises your existing columns (date, student, amount) and adds a few 💰 columns at the end.

What if a payment isn't recognised?

It goes to the "💰 Incoming payments" tab, where you assign it to a student from a list. Next time it is recognised automatically.

Does it replace the Bit or bank app?

No. Detection is a convenient tracking tool, not an accounting record or proof that money arrived. If in doubt, check the app.

Are WhatsApp reminders safe?

They are sent through GREEN API, an unofficial third-party service, so there is a risk the number may be restricted. The add-on sends few, spaced messages, only to parents with a balance. See section 9 of the Terms. You can also work without reminders.

How do I stop?

Simply don't renew, or tell us. To remove the add-on: Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons.
