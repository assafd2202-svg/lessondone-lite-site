9. WhatsApp through GREEN API

WhatsApp reminders are optional; the Service works without them. They are sent from your number through GREEN API, a third-party service not operated by WhatsApp or Meta, which connects your WhatsApp as a "linked device" (like WhatsApp Web).

9.1 Why there is a risk

WhatsApp's terms do not permit automated sending through unofficial services. WhatsApp may detect such sending and restrict or ban the number, temporarily or permanently, possibly without warning. The risk grows with many messages, recipients who have not saved your number, or spam reports. GREEN API itself states that this risk exists.

9.2 How LessonDone reduces the risk

Reminders go only to parents who already know you and have an open balance, at most 3 times per balance and no more than once every 48 hours, only Sunday to Thursday in the evening, spaced tens of seconds apart, and only after you approved the message text. This resembles a tutor sending reminders personally, not bulk messaging. We are not aware of a tutor banned because of this use, but no outcome can be guaranteed.

9.3 Recommendations

  • Use a number you can recover or replace.
  • Make sure parents know they may receive reminders from you, and have your number saved.
  • Stop messaging any parent who asks.
