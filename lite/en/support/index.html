Common questions

A payment did not appear in the sheet

Click "🔄 Update now" in the LessonDone sidebar. If it still does not appear, check that the automation app on your phone is active and allowed to read notifications. If in doubt, write to us.

Removing the add-on

In the spreadsheet: Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons → LessonDone Lite → Remove. To revoke access: myaccount.google.com/permissions. Your spreadsheet and its content remain.

Deleting your data

Write to us from the email you registered with. We delete registration data within 30 days, except data the law requires us to keep. See the Privacy Policy, section 11.

Cancelling

There is no automatic charge — simply don't renew. Cancelling within 14 days of your first payment gives a full refund. See the Terms, section 6.
